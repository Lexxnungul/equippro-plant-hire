# EquipPro Plant Hire: Material Design 3 Color System & Semantic Roles

## 1. Brand Key Colors & Rationale

The color system for EquipPro Plant Hire is anchored in the physical realities of heavy civil engineering and logistics in Lagos, Nigeria. The primary brand key is pinned to Tone 40 so the tonal palette generates around it rather than having its chroma absorbed or shifted by default HCT generation algorithms.

| Key Color Category | Palette Name | Key Hex (Tone 40) | Hue & Chroma Profile | Functional Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Primary** | Industrial Forge Rust | `#9A3412` | High Chroma, Warm Rust ($16^\circ$–$37^\circ$) | Pinned primary brand anchor; hero actions, active indicators |
| **2. Secondary** | Deep Umber Iron | `#7A584C` | Low Chroma, Muted Umber ($16^\circ$) | Recessive structural controls, secondary buttons, inactive chips |
| **3. Tertiary** | Warm Clay Sienna | `#8C4F2B` | Medium Chroma, Clay Sienna ($16^\circ$) | Contrasting highlights, badges, selected card outlines |
| **4. Neutral** | Warm Carbon Gray | `#615C59` | Minimal Chroma ($\approx 4$), Warm Industrial Gray | Screen surfaces, backdrops, modal scrims, high-emphasis text |
| **5. Neutral Variant** | Warm Slag Gray | `#655B54` | Subdued Chroma ($\approx 8$), Earthy Gray | Component outlines, field borders, divider lines, unselected chips |
| **6. Warning** | Industrial Amber | `#7D5700` | High Chroma, Gold-Amber ($\approx 75^\circ$) | Overstay caution, 250-hour service window alert, deposit warnings |
| **7. Error** | Crimson Alert | `#BA1A1A` | High Chroma, Cool Crimson ($\approx 25^\circ$) | Failed validation, database disconnects, critical stops |
| **8. Success** | Field Green | `#2E6C31` | High Chroma, Distinct Green ($\approx 140^\circ$) | Verified returns, cleared gate passes, confirmed payments |

## 3. Semantic Color Roles Across All 8 Palettes

In Material Design 3, color roles represent specific component responsibilities (jobs) rather than static color names. Every role pair used for text or interactive elements must maintain accessible contrast ratios.

### 3.1 Primary Roles (Brand Hero & High-Emphasis Controls)

| Role Token | Job Description | Light Scheme (Tone & Hex) | Dark Scheme (Tone & Hex) | Contrast Ratio (Pair) | WCAG Rating |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`primary`** | Main action fills, high-emphasis buttons, active icons | Tone 40: `#9A3412` | Tone 80: `#FFB59D` | 5.3:1 (against `#FFFFFF`) | AA Pass (Normal), AAA (Large) |
| **`on-primary`** | Text & icons resting on `primary` fill | Tone 100: `#FFFFFF` | Tone 20: `#5B1B03` | 5.3:1 (Light) / 8.4:1 (Dark) | AA / AAA Pass |
| **`primary-container`** | Background fill for prominent cards, FABs, and callouts | Tone 90: `#FFDBCF` | Tone 30: `#7A280B` | 13.9:1 (with `on-primary-container`) | AAA Pass |
| **`on-primary-container`** | High-contrast text & icons on `primary-container` | Tone 10: `#380D00` | Tone 90: `#FFDBCF` | 13.9:1 (Light) / 10.2:1 (Dark) | AAA Pass |
| **`inverse-primary`** | Interactive elements on `inverse-surface` (e.g. snackbar action) | Tone 80: `#FFB59D` | Tone 40: `#9A3412` | 7.1:1 (against `#32302E`) | AAA Pass |
